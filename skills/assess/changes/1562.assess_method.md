- the agent job scope inside local reusable workflows (issue #1562): a model named only in a
  called workflow's workflow-level `env:` reads the worst of the called jobs' scopes, each
  capped by the caller's, instead of `unread`, so a `read-all` caller now meets the
  least-privilege condition. A called workflow that also has its own trigger is counted once:
  each of its jobs appears under its own path at the worst of its own-trigger and caller-capped
  readings, and the caller's job is no longer a second entry. A caller whose called agent jobs
  have no write scope is no longer recorded `split: true`.
