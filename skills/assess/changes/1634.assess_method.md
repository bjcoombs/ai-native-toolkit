- API compatibility, a backgrounded apidiff re-raise (issue #1634): a re-raise sent to the
  background no longer counts, in a CI step or a followed script: a line with a bare `&` outside
  quotes (`apidiff a b | grep -q . && exit 1 &`, `[ -s o.txt ] && exit 1 &`,
  `test -n "$(apidiff a b)" && exit 1 &`), a `then`-branch line such as `exit 1 &`, or an `if`
  block closed by `fi &`. A background job's `exit 1` cannot fail the job.
