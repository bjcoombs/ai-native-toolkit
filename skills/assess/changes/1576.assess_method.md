- agent eval suites read GitLab hidden templates only as far as a running job takes them
  (issue #1576). An anchor alias or a `!reference` counts only in a visible job that runs: one
  in a comment, a `when: manual` job or another hidden job no longer makes the template a run,
  and a job merging a template (`<<: *evals`) that sets its own `script:` runs its own. A job
  whose every `rules:` entry is `when: never` or `when: manual` is not a run. A template a
  merged job extends stays readable for another job that takes it by alias or `!reference`,
  which read unmet after #1573.
