- the result gains `ceiling_step` and `held_above` (#1374). `ceiling_step` is the highest Step
  whose gates all hold once `next_step`'s blocking gates are met, every Step between held too;
  null when `next_step` is null. `held_above` lists the ids of the gates above the current Step
  that already hold, once each, in Step order; empty when none do. Both read gates only, so
  `step`, `next_step` and the tally are unchanged.
