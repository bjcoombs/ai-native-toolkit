- CI masking (issue #1622): a top-level `exit`, bare or with any status, ends its script
  body, so every later statement of that body is masked and earns no credit in any CI
  reader (`echo start; exit 0; pytest` no longer counts `pytest`). An `exit` inside an `if`,
  a loop, a `case`, a `{ }` group, a subshell or a function masks nothing after it.
