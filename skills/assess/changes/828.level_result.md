- declines and Step-blocking hazards (issue #828): a condition a decline record covers reads
  `declined` (never met, never counted, never holding a gate), and `declined[]` (`{id, source}`)
  lists them when any applies. A hazard condition carries `blocks` (its Step gate) when the model
  names one; while it is met the gate does not hold, with a blocker `{kind: "gate", gate,
  condition, state: "met", polarity: "hazard"}`. `hazards[]` (`{id, fix, gate?}`) lists each met
  hazard apart from the tally, with the check manifest's fix. The tally is unchanged.
